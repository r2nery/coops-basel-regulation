============================================================================================
C — multivariate operating space
============================================================================================
Generalised variance det(Sigma)^(1/k) over the residualised outcome vector, and
the trace, on standardised residuals so the outcomes are commensurable. The
generalised variance accounts for the correlation between margins; the trace does
not. A low generalised-variance ratio with a higher trace ratio would mean the
groups differ mainly in how their margins move together.

complete cases across all 6 outcomes: 6163 (2945 cooperative, 3218 bank)
