\subsection*{Classification rule}

Each outcome in Table~2 of the main text carries a tier. An outcome is \textit{null}
unless it is significant at five percent, with one sign, in all four specifications. A
significant outcome is \textit{stable} if it stays significant under every winsorisation
rule (1/99, 2.5/97.5, 5/95 and none), varies by less than a factor of two across those
rules, and has an average quantile coefficient between the twentieth and eightieth
percentiles of at least half the conditional mean. Otherwise it is \textit{stable in
sign}: the direction holds, but the tails or the shape of the distribution drive the
magnitude.

\subsection*{Small-cluster simulation}

With six treated clusters drawn at random from the cooperatives and relabelled banks as
the null, over 200 draws, the clustered test rejects a true null 16.5 percent of the time
at a nominal 5 percent level and 10 percent at a nominal 1 percent level; the restricted
wild cluster bootstrap rejects 10 percent and 0.5 percent. Bootstrap rejections at one
percent are therefore conservative, while those at five percent still over-reject. The
main comparison rests on about 130 treated clusters, where clustered and bootstrap
inference agree; the system splits in Table~\ref{tab:a8} report their treated cluster
counts.
